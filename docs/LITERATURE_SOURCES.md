## 2. Paper-writing format rules (11 Dec checklist and instructions)

Sources: `Final Paper Instructions`, `Final Paper Checklist_ ECON_V 490 003
2026W1 Seminar in Applied Economics.pdf`, and the Canvas page
https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/193437/pages/final-paper-checklist.

- Bibliography in AER / Chicago author-date style. **All datasets must appear
  in the bibliography too** — the five citation strings in
  `docs/DATA_CITATIONS.md` can be reused.
- Use present tense, cite papers rather than authors ("Galor (2015) argues",
  not "Omer Galor argues"), and do not put paper titles in the body text.
- Any quotation or reported statistic needs a page number: `[Galor (2015), p. 64]`.
- Canadian spelling.
- "et al." only for four or more authors; spell out all three authors on first
  use, then abbreviate.
- 1.5 line spacing, 12-point font, 2.5 cm margins, no more than three direct
  quotations in the whole paper.
- No raw Stata output anywhere in the paper; tables must be reformatted from the
  exported `.docx` files.
